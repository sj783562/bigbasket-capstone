# Data Story — BigBasket Category Performance (Jan–Jun 2026)

## Categories ahead of target

Three of six categories cleared their monthly revenue target over the six-month period:

- **Bakery**: ₹15,410 vs. a target of ₹12,000 — **+28.4%** above target, the strongest performer by percentage.
- **Household Essentials**: ₹21,715 vs. a target of ₹17,000 — **+27.7%** above target, and the single largest revenue contributor overall.
- **Personal Care**: ₹16,382 vs. a target of ₹15,500 — **+5.7%** above target, clearing the bar but by the smallest margin of the three.

## Categories behind target

Three categories fell short, split across the two "behind" tiers:

- **Dairy & Eggs** (Below Target - Watch): ₹14,090 vs. a target of ₹16,500 — **-14.6%**, just inside the 15%-shortfall watch threshold.
- **Snacks & Beverages** (Below Target - Critical): ₹10,895 vs. a target of ₹13,000 — **-16.2%**, past the critical threshold.
- **Fruits & Vegetables** (Below Target - Critical): ₹9,790 vs. a target of ₹12,000 — **-18.4%**, the weakest performer of all six categories both in absolute shortfall and percentage.

## Recommendations

**1. Prioritize Fruits & Vegetables for catalog and marketing investment.**
This category has both the largest percentage shortfall (-18.4%) and sits in the Critical tier. Given that Fruits & Vegetables is typically a high-frequency repeat-purchase category for a grocery platform, a shortfall this large points to either weak catalog visibility (product discoverability, search ranking) or under-investment in promotions relative to its target. This should be the top candidate for the category team's next marketing push or catalog expansion, since even a modest recovery toward the target would meaningfully close BigBasket's overall category revenue gap.

**2. Review supplier performance for Snacks & Beverages.**
Snacks & Beverages is also in the Critical tier (-16.2%), but unlike Fruits & Vegetables, this category typically depends more on product assortment breadth and supplier fulfillment than on foot-traffic/discovery alone. The revenue shortfall here is worth investigating from the supply side — whether specific SKUs are frequently out of stock, whether supplier lead times are affecting availability, or whether the current product mix under-represents high-demand items. A supplier and stock-availability audit for this category is a concrete next step distinct from the marketing-led fix recommended for Fruits & Vegetables.
